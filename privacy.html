Usage Dump: privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What the app collects

With the Usage access permission you grant, the app reads your phone's usage statistics: which apps were in use and when, screen on and off, unlocks, and notification events. It also records your phone's time zone, model and Android version.

Where it goes

The data is stored on your phone and, if you connect Google Drive, uploaded to a PhoneUsage folder in your own Google Drive. It is not sent anywhere else. The developer does not receive, see or store any of your data, and there is no server, analytics or advertising.

Google user data

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which lets it create files in your Drive and change the files it created, and nothing else. Its use of Google user data is limited to uploading your own usage files to your own Drive. It does not read any other files, does not share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data on the phone. Your files in Google Drive are yours: delete the PhoneUsage folder to remove them. You can withdraw the app's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
